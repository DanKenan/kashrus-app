How it works

Six short steps. Each picture shows what you will see on your phone.

  1. Sign in
  2. Face ID
  3. Your tasks
  4. Sign & Complete
  5. Updates
  6. Home screen
Step 1

Sign in once

Sign in with your email and password.

Tap Sign in at the top of this page. Type your email and your password. Then tap “Sign In to Shift Board”.

Step 2

Quick sign-in

Add Face ID or fingerprint.

After you sign in, the app asks: “Sign in faster next time?” Tap “Add Face ID or fingerprint”.

Next time, just tap “Quick sign-in”. No typing. Your password still works too.

Missed it? Open the menu, then Face ID or fingerprint. Add it on each phone you use.

Step 4

Sign & Complete

Did the check? Tap the button.

Find the task. Tap “Sign & Complete Inspection”.

It turns green with your name and the time. Your coordinator sees it right away.

Tapped by mistake? Tap it again to undo.

Step 5

Updates

Read announcements, shift notes and alerts.

  • Announcements: news from for the team.
  • Shift notes: leave a note for the next shift. Type it or record your voice.
  • Alerts: something wrong? Tap “Raise an alert” and add a photo. Your coordinators get it at once.

Step 6

Home screen

Add the app to your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap Share
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the menu ⋮
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen

Now open it from your home screen, like any app.
